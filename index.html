About Fluffy Knots

Fluffy Knots began with one ball of pink yarn and a lot of love. Today, every plushie, flower and pouch is still crocheted by hand — slowly, carefully, and always with a smile.

Whether you're treating yourself or picking a gift for someone special, we hope our little creations bring you the same joy we felt making them. Thank you for supporting a small handmade business. Stay cute, stay cozy! 🌸
